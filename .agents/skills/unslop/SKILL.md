# Unslop

Adapted from [pstack](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/main/pstack) (MIT). Scan for these patterns, then rewrite. Keep the meaning and tone.

- No trailing "-ing" phrases that add no fact ("ensuring...", "highlighting...").
- No vague attributions ("experts believe"). Name the source or delete.
- No AI vocabulary: additionally, crucial, delve, enhance, foster, landscape, pivotal, showcase, testament, underscore, vibrant, leverage, utilize, robust, seamless.
- Say "is" or "has", not "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features".
- No "not just X, but Y". State the point.
- Don't force ideas into threes.
- One name per thing. Repeat it instead of using synonyms.
- No em dashes or en dashes as connectors. Use a period or a comma.
- Don't bold every noun. No bold label that restates its line.
- Sentence case headings. No decorative emojis. Straight quotes.
- No chatbot phrases, praise openers or generic conclusions.
- Cut filler: "in order to" is "to"; delete "it is important to note that".
- At most one hedge.
- Say what it does, not how it feels. Name the mechanism or the number.
- One idea per sentence. Active voice; name the actor.
- No metaphors where a literal phrase exists ("north star", "flywheel", "surface").
